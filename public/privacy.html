Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what Aurora collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number, and city/area.
  • Delivery addresses you save.
  • Orders, cart, wishlist and chat messages with sellers.
  • Notification settings and, if you allow them, push-notification tokens.
  • Basic technical logs (IP address, device type) used for security and abuse prevention.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your card number or bank login details. Payments are made by bank transfer from your own banking app. We do not store your password; sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase).

How we use it

To create and secure your account, process and deliver orders, share what the seller needs to fulfil your order (your name, phone and delivery address), send order updates, prevent fraud and handle disputes.

Who we share it with

  • The seller of an item you order.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database and authentication), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email) and push-notification services.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in Settings. Your personal details, saved items and notifications are removed. Order and payment records needed for accounting and dispute handling are kept without your name.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted, and we monitor for abuse. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data under applicable law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act. Use Help & Support in the app to make a request.

Children

Aurora is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new date.
